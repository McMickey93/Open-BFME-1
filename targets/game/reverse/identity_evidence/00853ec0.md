# 0x00853EC0 is not GarrisonContain::xfer

The 72-byte body at `0x00853EC0` was landed as
`?xfer@GarrisonContain@@MAEXPAVXfer@@@Z` (a `__declspec(naked)` `__emit` lift,
`game/GameEngine/Source/GameLogic/Object/Contain/GarrisonContain_xfer_Thunk.cpp`),
but the real `GarrisonContain::xfer` lives at `0x0021DAA0` (403 bytes, `ret 4`
at `+0x190`), reached from `GarrisonContain`'s constructor at `0x0021D820`
(`??0GarrisonContain`, matched) via its installed vtable `0x010AB818` slot 3,
ILT `0x0000EAA7`.

The 0x00853EC0 body itself is constructor-shaped, not xfer-shaped: an SEH
prologue, `mov esi, ecx` (this into esi), zeroing `[esi+4]`, `[esi+8]` and
`[esi+0xC]`, pushing the literal `0x64` (100), storing `[esi+0x10]`, then
`call 0x00853510` and `mov eax, esi` (returns `this`). Decoding the `e8`
relative call at body offset 49 independently gives target `0x00853510`
(`next_ip 0x00853EF6 + rel32 0xFFFFF61A = 0x00853510`), which `functions.csv`
already carries as `_M_initialize_buckets` of a `gen-tgrid` hashtable
template — a callee an xfer/transfer method has no reason to invoke, and 72
bytes cannot hold the retail `GarrisonContain::xfer` body (team-ID lookup,
exception throw, and a 40-entry garrison-point loop that alone needs far more
than 72 bytes).

The name is retired to the address-derived `?dup_00853ec0@@YAXXZ`; the bytes,
RVA, size and source are unchanged, so byte coverage is preserved and no
identity claim beyond "some 72-byte body at this address" is asserted.
