# Bank name corrections: 0x004B2330 and 0x007921E0 (2026-10-12)

Two banked scratch reconstructions were converted into real TUs. Each bank
carried local names for helpers that the conversion now reaches through
retail's ILT entries; every renamed name is documented here and in
`targets/game/reverse/name_corrections.json`.

## 0x004B2330 ?Rva004B2330@@YAHPAVObject@@@Z

attempts/0x004b2330.cpp -> game/GameEngine/Source/GameClient/GUI/Rva004B2330.cpp

The bank was a scratch reconstruction of the TooltipText builder at 0x004B25D0
whose scratch classes modeled the called bodies locally. The 252-byte helper
conversion binds the same call sites through the recorded ILT entries
(0x0000EDC7, 0x0000DFC1, 0x00041295, 0x000012A8, 0x00031471) and spells each
body by its recorded address-qualified name, as ilt_guard and the
one-body-one-name rule require:

- Iterator004B25D0 -> Rva0037F220Iterator: the same 8-byte iterator struct; the
  conversion names it after the recorded body (rva0037F220, ILT 0x00031471)
  that returns it.
- RankLimit004B25D0 -> Gen_001b2030: the rank-limit getter is now typed as the
  recorded ledger body at 0x001B2030, called through ILT 0x000012A8.
- UnicodeString -> Rva0037E810Binding: the bank's STLport wide-string copy
  helper set (WideCopy00888400 plus inline UnicodeString ctors) existed for the
  builder's inlined string handling; the helper row needs none of it and types
  the flag gate at 0x0037E810 as a member-pointer union binding instead
  (CommandXlatVoice.cpp idiom).
- construct -> rva0037F190: WideCopy00888400::construct falls with that helper
  set; rva0037F190 is the recorded body (ILT 0x0000EDC7) that seeds the
  iterator walk.
- __declspec -> Rva004B2330Caller: token-alignment artifact; the conversion's
  new absent-from-retail caller anchor Rva004B2330Caller exists only to force
  the helper's private calling convention, as the retail caller at 0x004B25D0
  does, and deliberately stays address-qualified.
